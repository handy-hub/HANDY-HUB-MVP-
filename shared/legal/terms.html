4. Payments & Wallet

You pay through your Fidus X wallet. You add money to it with Mobile Money or a card through our payment provider, Paystack. A top-up is added to your wallet only after Paystack confirms the payment.

  • Callout fee. When you request a visit, the callout fee shown before you confirm is taken from your wallet.
  • Job price. After inspecting, the artisan sends you a quote. When you approve it, the quoted amount is taken from your wallet and held for the job.
  • Release to the artisan. The held amount is released to the artisan when the job is completed. If a job is still unconfirmed 7 days after you paid, the amount is released to the artisan if the job was marked done, and otherwise refunded to you. Money for a job under dispute stays held until Fidus X resolves the dispute.
  • Our commission. Fidus X keeps 15% of each amount released to an artisan. You pay only the fees and the price shown in the app before you confirm.
  • Refunds. A refund is credited to your Fidus X wallet as soon as it is decided.
  • Withdrawals. You can withdraw your wallet balance to a Mobile Money number you have topped up from, after confirming with your security PIN. Withdrawals are sent through Paystack; how long they take to arrive depends on the Mobile Money provider.

5. Cancellations

  • If nobody accepts your request in time, it ends and anything you paid for it is refunded to your wallet.
  • If you cancel before the visit has taken place, your callout fee is refunded to your wallet. If you cancel after the artisan has inspected, the callout fee is paid to the artisan for the visit. An emergency request can be cancelled until the artisan sets out.
  • If the artisan cancels, your callout fee is refunded to your wallet.
  • After you have approved a quote, contact support to cancel; the held amount is settled when the problem is resolved.

Fidus X charges no cancellation fee.
