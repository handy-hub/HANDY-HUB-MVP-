Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-28 · Last updated 28 October 2026

1. Information We Collect

From everyone: the information you provide (name, email, phone, the addresses where you want work done), usage data (searches, bookings, messages), and device information (operating system, app version, and a notification token).

From artisans, to verify identity and pay earnings: Ghana Card number, date of birth, residential address, emergency contact, and the Mobile Money account earnings are paid to. While an artisan is available for work, the app also records their precise location so that nearby requests can be offered to them.

Payments: the amounts and references of top-ups and withdrawals, and the Mobile Money numbers you top up from or save, so that withdrawals can only go back to a number you have paid from. Card details are entered with Paystack and never reach Fidus X.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to match customers with artisans, verify artisans, process payments and withdrawals, send service notifications, provide support, and improve our platform. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Sharing

Artisans' identity, payout and precise location data are visible only to the artisan and to Fidus X administrators. Customers see an artisan's public profile and an approximate area, not their exact location.

When a booking goes ahead, we share with the artisan what the job needs: your name, the job address, and your phone number while the booking is active (from acceptance until the job is done).

We use service providers to run Fidus X: Google Firebase (accounts, data storage, notifications), Paystack (payments and withdrawals), Cloudinary (profile photos) and Google Maps (addresses and maps). They process data on our behalf and have their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers. We use encryption and access controls to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your personal information at any time via the app. To request deletion of all your data, use the Delete Account option in Settings or email info.fidusx101@gmail.com. An account cannot be deleted while it still holds a balance, money held for a job, or an active booking.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your preferences (language, currency, theme) and to show screens you have visited before quickly. No advertising cookies are used.

7. Children's Privacy

Fidus X is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The version and date at the top of this page change whenever the text does, and we will notify you of significant changes via the app.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info.fidusx101@gmail.com.
