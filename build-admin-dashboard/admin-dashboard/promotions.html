Promotions drive the Customer App dashboard banner. Customer devices cache the banner, so a change can take up to ~10 minutes to reach everyone (background revalidation applies sooner, ~60s). The customer app also ships 3 hardcoded fallback slides that show only when no eligible Firestore promotion exists — these live in customer-app/js/adBanner.js and are not editable here. Only the top 3 eligible promotions (by priority) display at once.
